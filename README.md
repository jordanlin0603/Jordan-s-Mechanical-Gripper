# Jordan's Mechanical Gripper

A manually operated mechanical gripper designed and assembled in SolidWorks.

The goal of this project was to develop my SolidWorks skills while learning how a mechanical linkage can convert linear motion into symmetric gripping motion. The final design uses a center slider, two connecting links, and two pivoting fingers to open and close the jaws.

The gripper is operated manually using a guide pin attached to the center slider as a handle.

## Final Design

The completed assembly includes:

- Two pivoting gripper fingers
- Two connecting links
- Center slider
- Two grooved jaw pads
- Guide pins
- Four linkage pivot pins
- Two M6 main pivot bolts
- Manual slider handle
- Base plate with center clearance cutout
- Slider guide slot
- Angle limits to control finger travel

The mechanism was modeled and assembled in SolidWorks and tested by manually moving the center slider through its operating range.

Material was not specified because the focus of the project was CAD design, mechanical linkage development, and engineering documentation.

| Measurement | Value |

| Maximum jaw opening | 44.00 mm |

| Total jaw opening change | 42.95 mm |
| Center slider travel | 9.14 mm |

The final assembly uses a limit-angle mate with an operating range of approximately **70° to 90°**.
